# Figures for 1994-2018, read by eye from the Companies House scans (txt/<year>.txt, made by text recognition).
# Format: year item value. Before 2019 the page uses only these, never the automatic reader (the old layouts and the
# auditor's tick marks over digits confuse it). Each figure is the year's own report, checked against the next report's
# "previous year" column; figures.py checks that the totals add up and that each year's funds follow from the last.
#
# Items: income = all incoming resources; donations = gifts, covenants and legacies; interest; other;
# spending = total resources expended; gains = gains/losses not in income (revaluations, a gift of property);
# net = net movement in funds; funds = net assets at 31 March; buildings = tangible fixed assets;
# cash = cash at bank plus short-term deposits.
# restricted_in = income given (or earned) for a set purpose: the restricted column of the statement of financial
# activities (1995-2001 the "endowment funds" column, mostly building funds; 2010 includes a 500,000 endowment).
# Cost lines from the notes ("Analysis of expenditure" / "Direct charitable expenditure"):
# sp_heat = heat, light and rates (later: heat, light, water and council tax); sp_ins = insurance;
# sp_maint = maintenance; sp_depr = depreciation (wear on buildings); sp_food = food and welfare; sp_travel = travel.
# "checked" marks a figure the owner confirmed on the scan (16 Nov 2026). A figure still waiting for the owner's
# check is left out until it is confirmed.
