                 THE ENGLISH SANGHA TRUST LIMITED
                 (REGISTERED CHARITY NO. 231310)
                 REPORT OF THE DIRECTORS
                 The directors present their report together with financial
                 statements for the year ended 31 March 1996.
                Principal activities
                The company is principally engaged in charitable activities to
                promote and further the                                teaching of the Buddha by the provision
                maintenance and support of residential centres for Bhikkhus and
                other such persons as may be designated by the Sangha who have
                 undertaken the rules of training, and accomodate, maintain and
                 support aged and necessitous followers of the Buddha Sasana.
                Review of the year
                The surplus for the year amounted to £230,089. The Memorandum of
                Association forbids the payment of a dividend                                                       and the surplus
                has therefore been retained and transferred to Reserves to
                promote and further the activities of the company.
               Donations in kind
               The financial statements do not reflect the value of donations in
               kind.
               Directors
               The directors in office at the end of the year are listed below.
               Venerable Viradhammo Bhikkhu and Linda Maria Medhina retire by

               Director's responsibilities for the financial statements
                Company law requires the directors to prepare financial statements

               subject to any material departures disclosed and explained in the
               financial statements;
               Prepare the financial statements on the going concern basis unless
               it is inappropriate to presume that the company will continue in
               business.
               The directors are responsible for keeping proper accounting
               records, for safeguarding the assets of the company and for taking

                REPORT OF THE AUDITORS TO THE MEMBERS OF
                THE ENGLISH SANGHA TRUST LIMITED
                We have audited the financial statements on pages 7 to 14 and the
                accounting policies set out on page 6.
               Respective responsibilities of directors and auditors
               As described on page 3, the company's directors are responsible for
                the preparation of financial statements. It is our responsibility
                to form an independent opinion, based on our audit, on those
                statements and to report our opinion to you.
                Basis of opinion
               We conducted our audit in accordance with Auditing Standards issued
               by the Auditing Practices Board except that our work was limited as
               explained below.
               An audit includes examination, on a test basis, of evidence relevant
               to the amounts and disclosures in the financial statements. It also
               includes an assessment of the significant estimates and judgements
               made by the directors in the preparation of the financial statements,
               and of whether the accounting policies are appropriate to the
               company's circumstances, consistently applied and adequately disclosed.
               We planned and performed our audit so as to obtain all the information
               and explanations which we considered necessary in order to provide us
               with sufficient evidence to give reasonable assurances that the
               financial statements are free from material misstatement, whether
               caused by fraud or other irregularity or error. However the evidence
               available to us was limited because of the matters referred to below.
              This charity derives a substantial proportion of its income from
              voluntary donations. These cannot be fully controlled until they are
               entered in the accounting records, and are not therefore susceptible
               co independent audit verification. This limitation, which is common to
               nany other charities of similar size and orqanisation is outside the
               control of the directors.
               As explained in the accounting policies note on page 6, no depreciation
               is charged on freehold buildings, as the company's directors consider
               their valuation to be considerably in excess of their book value.
               There is no evidence available to enable us to gain assurance as to
               the validity of the assumption.
              In forming our opinion we also evaluated the overall adequacy of the
              presentation of information in the financial statements.

                THE ENGLISH SANGHA TRUST LIMITED
                ACCOUNTING POLICIES
                FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 MARCH 1996
                The financial statements have been prepared under the historical
                cost convention, except for one freehold property, which is shown
                at its valued amount.
               The principal accounting policies of the company have remained
                unchanged from the previous year and are set out below.
               Donations
               The financial statements disclose                                    income received in the form of
               donations of money. They do not disclose donations received in
               kind. Financial donations are accounted for once recorded in the
               books of the company. The tax reclaimable on covenanted income is
               recorded on a "as received" basis.
               Investment income
               Investment income is interest received on short term deposits.
               Depreciation
               Depreciation is charged to write down the cost of the tangible
               fixed assets, excluding freehold property, over their expected
               useful lives. Assets which are considered obsolete are written
               off immediately.
                     The rates are applied on a straight line basis as follows:-
                     Motor vehicles
                     Plant, machinery and equipment
                     Furniture, fixtures and fittings - 20%
               Individual items of the above categories of assets which cost
               £250 or less are written off in the year of acquisition.
              No depreciation is provided on freehold property. It is the
              company's practice to maintain these assets in a continual
               state of sound repair and to extend and make improvements
               thereto from time to time and accordingly the directors consider
              that the lives of these assets are so long and residual values
              are so high, that the depreciation is insignificant.
              Property improvements, maintenance and repair
              Expenditure on projects clearly planned to enhance the capital
              values of properties is capitalised. All other expenditure on
              property maintenance and repair is written off as expenditure
             in the year.
              General Reserve
              The General Reserve is used to support the overall charitable
              objectives of the company as detailed in note 1 of the directors'
              report.
