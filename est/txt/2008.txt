                      backgrounds The monasteries are open on a daily basis for meditation and puja Meditation classes are run at
                      the weekend During the year public talks and lectures are given, including the series of Sunday Talks at
                      Amaravatı Lay supporters can also receive the Forest Sangha Newsletter, which is published on a quarterly
                      basis,  with    around 5,000 subscribers                 The Newsletter is also available                    on the      internet at

                      Amaravatı and Cittaviveka

                     community A range of weekend 5, 6 and 10 retreats were run, attended by a total of around 700 retreatants
                     In addition to retreats led by monastics, a programme of lay led retreats and days of practice were run by the
                      Amaravatı Lay Buddhist Association (ALBA), a lay Buddhist group affilated to Amaravatı Buddhist Monastery
                      An annual famıly camp was also run by lay Buddhists at Amaravatı monastery in August and two weekend
                      events were also held
                      Members of the monastic community led retreats elsewhere in the UK and overseas, including the US, Spain,
                      Italy, Ireland, Portugal, and Thailand Members of the Sangha also visit groups to give talks on Buddhism,
                      medıtation and Buddhıst practice Addıtionally Sangha members visıt Buddhist prison inmates, carry out
                     hospital visits and attend funerals of supporters

                      monasteries at Amaravatı and Cittaviveka Teachers, other relıgious groups and universıty students are also
                     regular visitors The charty also provides materal for the study of Buddhism and life at the monastery to these
                     visitors and to others who request them

                     The charity has two websites www amaravatı org and www cittaviveka org The Amaravatı website includes

                      Reserves Policy
                     The reserves policy is reviewed annually by the finance sub-committee and more often should significant
                     changes in circumstances arise The sub-committee's recommendations are ratified at Trustees' Meeting
                      The trustees have established a policy whereby the unrestricted funds not committed to tangible fixed assets
                     should be sufficient to cover             the equivalent of the greater of (i) approximately three months' typical
                     expenditure, or (II) two years' core essential costs, assuming that there is reduced but not nil income                            The
                     trustees have calculated on this basis that the Trust should aim to have at least £150,000 retained as
                     unrestricted free reserves
                     As at 31 March 2008, free reserves (ie excluding those funds designated or committed to fixed assets) stood at
                     £173,926 It is anticipated that the free reserves will decrease during the next year
                     Full details of the charty's reserves are provided in note 15 to the financial statements
