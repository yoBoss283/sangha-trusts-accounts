                1 1 Basis of preparation
                       The accounts have been prepared under the historcal cost convention except for the valuation of

                1 2 Incoming resources
                       Incoming resources are accounted for on a receivable basis in the period in which the company is
                       entitled to receipt and the amount can be measured with reasonable certainty                     Income is deferred only
                       when the charty has to fulfil conditions before becoming entitied to it or where the donor specifies that it
                       be expended in future periods
                1 3 Resources expended
                       The policy for including items within the relevant activity categories of resources expended is on an
                       accruals basis Expenditure is recognised when a liability is incurred
                       In particular the policy for including items within costs of generating funds, chantable activities and

                       Charitable activities include expenditure associated with promoting the teachings of the Buddha and
                       include both the direct costs and support costs relating to this activity

                                     ANNUAL REPORT HARNHAM BUDDHIST MONASTERY
                                     For the year ending 31 December 2012
                                     The trustees of Harnham Buddhist Monastery take this opportunity, during the
                                     month of Vesakha Puja, to again offer our best wishes to the extended community of
                                     friends and supporters and to report on the activities of the monastery during 2012
                                     The Trust
                                     Harnham Buddhist Monastery Trust (HBMT) is the governing body responsible for
                                     the stewardship of Harnham Buddhist Monastery The Magga Bhavaka Trust
                                     remains operative for the time being as it holds the lease to No 2 Harnham Hail
                                     Cottages which houses the main monastery reception room and kitchen The
                                     trustees are currently arranging for the lease to be transferred into the name of
                                     HBMT This process is in its final stages and once completed the MBT may be
                                     wound up The possibility of purchasing the freehold to secure the longer term future
                                     of the monastery is still being explored
                                     Activities
                                     The last year has seen further significant activity and development at Harnham Lake
                                     at the foot of the hill from the monastery The boardwalk, constructed to enable an
                                     easy circumnavigation of the lake, has almost been completed Three kutis and a
                                     storage barn have also been erected and are in use with only the final touches
                                     required for completion Some of the monastic community have already made use of
                                     this much needed extra accommodation braving the inclement weather to stay in
                                     these sturdy huts The landscaping of the surrounding area has been progressed
                                     and more trees have been planted as part of the on-going project The trustees
                                     continue to be inspired by the generous donations which have gone towards making
                                     this important and enhancing development possible We are also happy to report
                                     that a dedicated car parking area at the entrance gate to the lake area has been
                                     completed which should alleviate car parking congestion especially on festival days
                                     and help foster good relations with our neighbours Other major work carried out
                                     during the year included the refurbishment and re-roofing of the Byre (which serves
                                     as accommodation for up to 4 community members) and part of the adjacent
                                     covered walkway
                                     The Monastery Committee
                                      This group of four lay supporters and four community members acts in the day-to-
                                      day management of the monastery They are in effect a sub-committee to the Trust
                                      and see to such matters as car insurance, organizing festivals, liaising between the
                                      laity and the monastery residents The group meets about every six weeks
                                      depending on the extent of activities The structure of the committee has been
                                      modified during the year to include honorary members from the Thai and Sri Lankan
