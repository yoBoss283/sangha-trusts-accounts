                     Accounting policies
               1.1 Basis of preparation
                      The accounts have been prepared under the historical cost convention
                      The accounts have been prepared in accordance with applicable accounting standards, the Statement of
                      Recommended Practice, "Accounting and Reporting by Charities", issued in March 2005 and the
                      Companies Act 2006
               1 2 Incoming resources
                     Incoming resources are accounted for on a receivable basis in the period in which the company is
                      enttled to receipt and the amount can be measured with reasonable certainty                      Income is deferred only
                      when the charty has to fulfil conditions before becoming entitled to it or were the donor specifies that it
                      be expended in future periods
               1.3 Resources expended
                      The policy for including items within the relevant activity categories of resources expended is on an
                      accruals basis Expenditure is recognised when a liability is incurred
                      In particular the policy for including items within costs of generating funds, chantable activities and
                      governance costs is
                      Charitable activities include expenditure associated with promoting the teachings of the Buddha and
                      include both the direct costs and support costs relating to this activity
                      Governance costs include those costs incurred in the governance of the charty and its assets and are
                      primarily associated with the constitutional and statutory requirements
                      Support costs have been split between chantable and governance costs
               1 4 Tangible fixed assets and depreciation
                      Tangible fixed assets other than freehold land are stated at cost less depreciation Depreciation is
                      provided at rates calculated to write off the cost less estimated residual value of each asset over its
                      expected useful life, as follows
                      Freehold property                                      1% Straight Line
                      Fixtures, fittings & equipment                          25% Straight Line
               1 5 Leasing and hire purchase commitments
                      Rentals payable in respect of operating leases where substantially all the benefits and risks of ownership
                      remain with the lessor are charged to the Statement of Financial Activities as incurred
               1.6 Investment Income
                      Bank interest is included in the income and expenditure account on a receivable basis
               1.7 Funds structure policy
                      The chanty maintains a general unrestricted fund which represents funds which are expendable at the

                      finance both working capital and capital investment All funds are unrestricted

                  Directors' Report
                  Harnham Buddhist Monastery Trust (HBMT) is the successor body of The Magga
                  Bhavaka Trust (MBT) Registered Charity No 326695 HBMT has been set up as a
                  chartable company with a clear objective to support the Theravadan Forest Sangha
                  Monastic tradıtion in Britain The majority of trustees of Magga Bhavaka Trust are also
                  Directors of the HBMT
                  HBMT took over as the main operating body for Harnham Buddhist Monastery on 1st
                  January 2010 The majority of MBT's current assets (money in bank) were transterred to
                  HBMT on 1st January 2010 Fixed assets (I e buildings) remained with MBT whilst
                  advice was taken and negotiations took place to convey the properties to HBMT
                  Conveyance was completed in October 2010
                  The ownership of the lease on No 2 Harnham Hill Cottages remains with MBT and the
                 trustees are currently exploring their legal position regarding the potential right to
                  purchase the property The governing bodies of MBT and HBMT continue to work in
                 tandem, with a view to formally winding up MBT within the next two years Meetings of
                  the two bodies are held concurrently
                  The most significant new activity of HBMT over the past year has been the purchase of
                  Harnham Lake and the surrounding land situated at the bottom of the hill from the
                  monastery This purchase was made possible by the generous legacy of Jody Higgs, a
                 long standing supporter and Trustee of the monastery who passed away in 2008 The
                  new property, approximately 4 ½ acres of Lake and 5 acres of land, provides the
                  community with much needed outdoor space and the opportunity to establish kutis for
                  private retreat and contemplation Conveyance was completed on 16iẠạiSeptember 2010,
                  coinciding with Ajahn Munindo's birthday
